## Project Title

EV Charging Infrastructure Intelligence Dashboard

### Phase 1: Data Cleaning & Pre-processing

* Handle missing State and City values.
* Standardize Country names.
* Clean and normalize Operator names.
* Split Connector Types into individual values.
* Correct inconsistent Status values.
* Validate Latitude and Longitude coordinates.
* Remove duplicate charging stations.
* Create Charging Capacity categories.

### Phase 2: Calculated Columns

* Station Age
* Connector Category
* Operational Flag
* Connector Capacity Group
* Country Region

### Phase 3: DAX Measures

* Total Stations
* Operational Stations
* Non-Operational Stations
* Total Connectors
* Average Connectors per Station
* Operational %
* Top Operator

### Phase 4: Dashboard Pages

#### Page 1 – Executive Overview

* KPI Cards:

  * Total Stations
  * Operational %
  * Total Connectors
* Interactive Map of Charging Stations
* Stations by Country
* Operational vs Non-Operational Stations

#### Page 2 – Infrastructure Analysis

* Connector Type Distribution
* Operator-wise Analysis
* Station Capacity Analysis
* Country-wise Comparison
* Top Operators

#### Page 3 – Station Details

* Drillthrough by Country, Operator, and Station
* Detailed Station Information Table
* Connector Details and Specifications
